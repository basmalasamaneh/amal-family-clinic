---
name: Visit authoring scope
description: Approved first-version scope for doctor visit notes and corrections.
---

The user approved a simple visit-note editor inside the patient card: online-only saving, one note field plus the visit time, and appended corrections instead of replacing or deleting the original.

**Why:** Keep documentation simple while preserving medical history and the existing consent boundary. Shared read access must not become authority to write on another clinic's behalf.

**How to apply:** Do not introduce offline clinical drafts or automatic appointment completion without separate approval. New-patient demographics and appointments do not establish the trusted treating-doctor relationship; any first-visit assignment workflow needs explicit authorization design.
