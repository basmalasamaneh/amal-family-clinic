---
name: Source-note extraction conservatism
description: Why the bilingual extractor deliberately favors missed mentions and retains historical manual evidence.
---

Keep keyword flags explicitly unconfirmed and the dictionary deliberately small.
When scope is uncertain, suppress the whole clause rather than guessing which
mention a negation or family subject qualifies.

**Why:** This product requires deterministic source evidence, not clinical inference.
More keyword matches are not inherently better and must not be described as
comprehensive clinical coverage.

**How to apply:** Expand language coverage only with positive and exclusion tests
and a dictionary version change; preserve the original evidence alongside labels.
Do not silently overwrite historical manual evidence when reconciling derived flags.
